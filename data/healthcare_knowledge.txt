Diabetes

Diabetes is a chronic condition that affects how the body processes blood glucose.

Common symptoms may include increased thirst, frequent urination, increased hunger, unexplained weight loss, fatigue, and blurred vision.

People with symptoms or concerns about diabetes should consult a qualified healthcare professional for proper evaluation and diagnosis.


Hypertension

Hypertension means that blood pressure is consistently higher than the recommended range.

High blood pressure may not always cause noticeable symptoms. Some people may experience headaches, dizziness, or other symptoms, but symptoms alone cannot confirm hypertension.

Regular blood pressure monitoring and consultation with a healthcare professional are important for proper evaluation.


Common Cold

The common cold is a viral infection that commonly affects the nose and throat.

Common symptoms include a runny nose, sore throat, coughing, sneezing, and mild fatigue.

Most common colds improve with time, but people with severe, persistent, or concerning symptoms should seek medical advice.



Dengue

Dengue is a viral infection transmitted by infected mosquitoes.

Common symptoms may include high fever, headache, muscle and joint pain, nausea, vomiting, and rash.

People with suspected dengue should seek medical evaluation for proper assessment and care.



Asthma

Asthma is a condition that affects the airways and can make breathing difficult.

Common symptoms may include wheezing, coughing, shortness of breath, and chest tightness.

People with breathing difficulties or persistent symptoms should consult a qualified healthcare professional for proper evaluation.
